# 翻譯結果

## 原文

今天天氣真好，我們去公園散步吧，順便學習一些成語，像是畫蛇添足、守株待兔。

---

## Translation

The weather is really nice today. Let's go for a walk in the park and learn some Chinese idioms along the way, like "drawing legs on a snake" and "waiting by a tree stump for a hare."

---

## 特殊詞彙

### 1. 畫蛇添足 (huà shé tiān zú)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| **字面意思** | To draw legs on a snake |
| **實際含義** | Ruining something by adding unnecessary extras; overdoing it |
| **詞性** | 成語 (idiom) |
| **發音** | huà shé tiān zú ≈ /hwǎ shɤ́ tjɛ́n tsǔ/ |

**例句：**
> 這份報告已經很完整了，再加這些附錄簡直是畫蛇添足。
>
> *The report is already complete — adding these appendices is like drawing legs on a snake.*

**趣味用法：**
> "That filter you added to the photo? Total 畫蛇添足 — the original was perfect."

---

### 2. 守株待兔 (shǒu zhū dài tù)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| **字面意思** | To guard a tree stump waiting for a hare |
| **實際含義** | Relying on luck instead of taking action; waiting passively for opportunities |
| **詞性** | 成語 (idiom) |
| **發音** | shǒu zhū dài tù ≈ /shǒu dʒū dài tù/ |

**例句：**
> 與其守株待兔等獵人上門，不如主動出擊去尋找客戶。
>
> *Instead of waiting by the stump for a hare to show up, you should proactively go find clients.*

**趣味用法：**
> "Refreshing your inbox every 5 seconds waiting for a reply? That's giving major 守株待兔 energy."

---

### 3. 成語 (chéng yǔ)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| **字面意思** | Established saying |
| **實際含義** | Chinese four-character idiomatic expressions, often derived from classical literature or historical anecdotes |
| **詞性** | 名詞 (noun) / 專業術語 (linguistic term) |
| **發音** | chéng yǔ ≈ /tʂɤ̌ŋ ỳ/ |

**例句：**
> 學習成語是了解中文文化的重要途徑。
>
> *Learning chéngyǔ is an important way to understand Chinese culture.*

**趣味用法：**
> "There are over 20,000 成語 in Chinese. Most native speakers only know a few hundred. We're all just pretending."
