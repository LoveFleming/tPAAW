## 2. Natural Translation

**Hot / Red-hot / In full swing**

The word「火熱」carries multiple layers of meaning. Depending on the context, it can translate to:

- **Hot** — describing weather, temperature, or something literally hot.
- **Red-hot / Heated** — describing intense emotions, competition, or situations.
- **In full swing / Booming** — describing a popular, trending, or actively ongoing event.

**Hot**

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
> `Things are + [adjective] + right now.`

This pattern is great for describing the current intensity or trend of something.

> Things are **red-hot** right now in the tech industry.

## 6. Speaking Version

> "It's really hot stuff right now." / "That's blowing up big time."

In casual conversation, native speakers might use **"blowing up"** (trending / going viral) or **"hot stuff"** (something popular and exciting) to express the energy behind「火熱」.

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ **Wrong:**
> This movie is very *fire hot*.

Many learners translate「火熱」literally as "fire hot," which sounds unnatural in English.

✅ **Correct:**
> This movie is **red-hot** right now.

Use **"red-hot"**, **"booming"**, or **"trending"** instead of a literal word-for-word translation.
