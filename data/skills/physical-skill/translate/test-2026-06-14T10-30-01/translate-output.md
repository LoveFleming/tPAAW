# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text
挑釁

## 2. Natural Translation
Provocation

## 3. Simple Translation
Picking a fight

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| provoke | 挑釁、激怒（動詞） | Don't provoke him — he has a short temper. |
| provocation | 挑釁行為（名詞） | He acted in self-defense after repeated provocation. |
| provocative | 挑釁性的（形容詞） | She made a provocative comment during the meeting. |
| pick a fight | 找碴、挑釁 | He's always picking a fight with someone at the bar. |
| antagonize | 激怒、使對立 | Try not to antagonize your coworkers on the first day. |
| goad | 煽動、刺激 | They goaded him into accepting the challenge. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
He is trying to ___ me into ___.

**Example:**
He is trying to provoke me into losing my temper.

## 6. Speaking Version

He's just trying to pick a fight — don't take the bait.

## 7. Common Mistake

Don't say: "He is challenging to me."
Say: "He is provoking me." or "He is picking a fight with me."

Explanation: "challenging to me" sounds like something is difficult for you, not that someone is trying to provoke you. Use "provoke" or the phrasal verb "pick a fight" when you mean someone is deliberately trying to make you angry.

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

Don't let his provocation get under your skin.
