# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text
主權

## 2. Natural Translation
Sovereignty

## 3. Simple Translation
Sovereignty — the supreme authority of a state to govern itself.

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| sovereignty | 主權 | Every nation has the right to sovereignty. |
| sovereign | 主權的；擁有主權的 | A sovereign state makes its own laws without outside control. |
| supreme authority | 最高權力 | The constitution grants the court supreme authority in legal disputes. |
| self-governance | 自治；自我治理 | Self-governance is a core principle of modern democracy. |
| territorial integrity | 領土完整 | The treaty guaranteed the country's territorial integrity. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
___ exercises sovereignty over ___.

**Example:**
The indigenous community exercises sovereignty over its ancestral lands.

## 6. Speaking Version

Sovereignty — basically, it means a country's right to rule itself, to make its own decisions without anyone else telling it what to do.

## 7. Common Mistake

Don't say: "sovereignty right" or "a sovereignty country."
Say: "the right to sovereignty" or "a sovereign country."

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

Sovereignty is the supreme authority of a state to govern itself.
