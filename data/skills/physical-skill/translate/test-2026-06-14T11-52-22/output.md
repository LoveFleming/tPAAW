# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text
盟友

## 2. Natural Translation
Ally

## 3. Simple Translation
A friend who helps you — especially during hard times or conflicts.

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| ally | 同盟者；盟友 | France and Britain were allies during World War II. |
| alliance | 聯盟；同盟關係 | The two companies formed a strategic alliance. |
| align with | 與……結盟；與……立場一致 | Several senators aligned with the president on the trade bill. |
| mutual | 互相的；共同的 | The treaty established mutual defense obligations. |
| loyalty | 忠誠；忠心 | The king rewarded the loyalty of his closest allies. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
___ allied with ___ to ___.

**Example:**
The small nation allied with a powerful neighbor to defend its borders.

## 6. Speaking Version

"Ally" is basically a stronger word for "friend" — but it's used when people or countries work together for a common goal, especially in politics, war, or business. You'd say: "She's not just a friend, she's an ally."

## 7. Common Mistake

Don't say: "He is my alliance."
→ Say: "He is my ally." *(alliance = the relationship; ally = the person)*

Don't say: "We made an ally."
→ Say: "We formed an alliance." *(you form an alliance, not make an ally)*

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

A true ally stands with you not just in victory, but in adversity.
