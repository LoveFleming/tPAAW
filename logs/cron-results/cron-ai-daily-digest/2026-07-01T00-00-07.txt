### Sentence Patterns

**Pattern 1: 表「標誌著某項轉變」— S + mark(s) + a shift / transition / milestone**
- **Structure**: [Subject] marks [a significant shift/milestone] in [field/concept]
- **From news**: "This marks the transition of AI from a conversational assistant toward an autonomous agent capable of executing complex tasks."
- **Your sentence**: The launch of the country's first AI regulatory framework marks a major milestone in digital governance.

**Pattern 2: 表「允許／使能夠」— S + enable(s) + someone + to + V**
- **Structure**: [Subject] enables [users/developers/businesses] to [action] with/at/by...
- **From news**: "The new model enables developers to run state-of-the-art inference on a single consumer-grade GPU."
