# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text
火熱

## 2. Natural Translation
In high demand / Intense

## 3. Simple Translation
Hot / Popular

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| in high demand | 需求量大、很搶手 | Skilled engineers are in high demand right now. |
| intense | 強烈的、極度的 | The competition in the tech industry is intense. |
| hot | 熱門的、流行的 | AI is a hot topic this year. |
| red-hot | 極度火熱的、非常活躍的 | The real estate market is red-hot right now. |
| passionate | 熱情的、充滿激情的 | She is passionate about her work. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
___ is in high demand.

**Example:**
Bilingual talent is in high demand in multinational companies.

## 6. Speaking Version

It's super hot right now. / It's really popular.

## 7. Common Mistake

Don't say: "It is fire hot." (literal translation)
Say: "It is really hot right now." or "It is in high demand."

Note: "火熱" is context-dependent. For market trends, use "hot" or "in high demand." For emotions, use "passionate" or "intense."

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

Skilled engineers are in high demand right now.
