# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

火熱

## 2. Natural Translation

Blazing hot / Red-hot / Fiery

## 3. Simple Translation

Very hot / Extremely popular

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 火熱 (huǒ rè) | 1. Physically blazing hot; 2. Extremely popular or trending | 這款手機在市場上非常**火熱**，一上市就銷售一空。 |
| red-hot | Extremely hot (physically); also used to describe something very popular or intense | The red-hot housing market shows no signs of cooling down. |
| trending | Currently very popular or widely discussed on social media / in public | The new song has been trending on TikTok for weeks. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

- **Pattern:** [Subject] + is/are + [adjective] + in + [place/context]
- **Example:** This product is **very hot** in the market right now.

## 6. Speaking Version

This thing is on fire right now! Everyone's talking about it.

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ **Wrong:** This product is firing hot. (逐字直譯「火熱」)
✅ **Correct:** This product is selling like hotcakes / This product is red-hot right now.

說明：「火熱」在中文裡可指物體溫度高或人氣高，但英文不能逐字直譯為 "firing hot"。根據語境，可以選用 "red-hot"（人氣極高）、"trending"（流行中）或 "selling like hotcakes"（熱銷）等更自然的表達。

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

Strike while the iron is hot — 打鐵要趁熱。
