# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

火熱

## 2. Natural Translation

Blazing hot / Trending

## 3. Simple Translation

Very hot / Very popular

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 火熱 (huǒ rè) | blazing hot; trending; fervent | 這款新手機在市場上**火熱**銷售，一機難求。 |
| trending | currently popular or widely discussed | The **trending** topic on social media right now is the new product launch. |
| fervent | having or displaying passionate intensity | His **fervent** enthusiasm for the project inspired the whole team. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:** Subject + is trending (火熱的) + in/on + place/time

**Example:** 「這款遊戲在亞洲市場火熱銷售。」
→ "This game is trending in the Asian market."

## 6. Speaking Version

"It's on fire right now." / "People are going crazy for it."

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ **錯誤用法：** 「This product is fire-hot selling.」
— 直接將「火熱」字面直譯為 "fire-hot"，不符合英文慣用表達。

✅ **修正：** 「This product is selling like hotcakes.」
— 英文慣用語 "sell like hotcakes" 表示銷售火熱、暢銷。

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

> When something is **火熱**, in English we say it's *on fire* or *trending* — not literally burning, but burning up the charts!
