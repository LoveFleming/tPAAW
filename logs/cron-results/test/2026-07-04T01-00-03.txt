# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

火熱

## 2. Natural Translation

Blazing hot / Red-hot

## 3. Simple Translation

Very popular / Extremely hot

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 火熱 (huǒ rè) | Describes something extremely popular or high-intensity | 這款商品在市場上**火熱**到供不應求。 |
| Red-hot | Extremely popular or successful | The red-hot startup just secured another round of funding. |
| trending | Currently popular or widely discussed | This topic is currently trending on social media. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:** [Subject] + 火熱 + [到/得] + [result/state]

**Example:** 這款 App 火熱到登上排行榜第一名。

## 6. Speaking Version

It's on fire right now. / Everyone's talking about it.

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ *Wrong:* Using 「火熱」 to directly describe literal temperature (e.g., 這杯咖啡很火熱)
✅ *Correct:* 「火熱」 is figurative, meaning "trending" or "red-hot" — e.g., 這首歌最近很火熱 → This song is trending right now

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

🔥 When something is 火熱, it's not just hot — it's on fire with popularity.
