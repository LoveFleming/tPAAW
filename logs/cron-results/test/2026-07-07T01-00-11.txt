## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern
**Pattern:** [Subject] + is + [adjective] + in + [place/context]

**Example:** This product is trending in the market.

## 6. Speaking Version
It's blowing up right now! / Everyone's talking about it!

## 7. Common Mistake
**錯誤用法:** "He is very hot in the market."（這樣說聽起來像在說這個人很性感，而不是他的東西受歡迎。）

**修正:** "His product is trending in the market."（要表達受歡迎時，用 "trending" 或 "popular" 修飾事物本身，而非人。）
