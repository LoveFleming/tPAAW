# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

火熱

## 2. Natural Translation

Scorching hot

## 3. Simple Translation

Very hot

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scorching | 極熱的、灼熱的 | The sun is scorching today. |
| blazing | 熾熱的、猛烈的 | A blazing fire kept us warm all night. |
| sizzling | 滾燙的、熱得發滋響的 | The sizzling steak smelled amazing. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

- **Pattern:** `It's scorching + (weather/time/place)`
- **Example:** It's scorching outside right now.

## 6. Speaking Version

It's burning hot out there!

## 7. Common Mistake

- ❌ The weather is very fire. （把「火」直譯為 fire）
- ✅ The weather is scorching hot. （用 scorching 表達「火熱」的熱度）

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

It's scorching hot outside — don't forget your sunscreen.
