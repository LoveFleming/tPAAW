# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

火熱

## 2. Natural Translation

**Hot / Trending**

「火熱」是一個多義的形容詞。最常見的意思是「非常受歡迎、正當紅」，英文可以用 **trending** 或 **hot** 來表達；它也可以指「氣氛熱烈、激烈」(heated / intense)，或是字面上的「灼熱」(scorching / red-hot)。

## 3. Simple Translation

**Very popular** or **on fire** 🔥

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **trending** | 正在流行；超夯、火熱 | This song is **trending** on social media right now. |
| **red-hot** | 極度火紅的；極受歡迎的 | The company became a **red-hot** startup after the funding round. |
| **heated** | 激烈的；熱烈的（討論、爭論） | They had a **heated** debate about the new policy. |
| **on fire** | 狀態絕佳；超級火紅（口語） | Her career has been **on fire** since she released that album. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
`[Subject] is + [trending / hot / on fire]` — 用來形容某人或某事物正當紅、非常受歡迎。

**Example:**
> This restaurant is **trending** on every food blog in town.

## 6. Speaking Version

> "It's totally **blowing up** right now!" 😎

（口語中常用 **blow up** 來表達「突然爆紅、變得超火熱」的意思，比 trending 更生活化。）

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ **錯誤用法：** "The movie is **fire hot**."
> 學生常把「火」+「熱」逐字翻譯，造成中式英文。

✅ **正確用法：** "The movie is **red-hot**." 或 "The movie is **trending**."
> 英文中不需要把 fire 和 hot 拆開組合；用單一的形容詞 red-hot 或 trending 更自然道地。

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

> **"Stay close to what's trending, but never lose your own fire."**
> （靠近潮流，但別失去自己的熱情。）
