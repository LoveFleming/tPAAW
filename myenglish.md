# Translation Learning Output

## 1. Original Text

解凍資金

## 2. Natural Translation

Unfreeze funds

## 3. Simple Translation

Release the locked money

## 4. Key Vocabulary

| Word / Phrase | Meaning | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unfreeze | 解凍；解除（資金、帳戶等）的限制 | The government decided to **unfreeze** the frozen assets. |
| funds | 資金；款項（通常指可運用的錢） | The company needs additional **funds** to complete the project. |
| frozen assets | 凍結資產（被限制動用的財產） | The court ordered the release of all **frozen assets**. |
| release | 釋放；解除限制使其可動用 | We will **release** the payment once the contract is signed. |

## 5. Useful Sentence Pattern

**Pattern:**
`unfreeze + Noun (funds / assets / accounts)` — 動詞「解凍」直接接受詞，表示解除對某筆資金或資產的限制。

**Example:**
- The central bank **unfroze 2 billion in funds** to stabilize the market.

## 6. Speaking Version

"Free up the money." / "Unlock those funds."

## 7. Common Mistake

❌ **錯誤：** "Melt the funds."
> 直接把「解凍」逐字翻成 "melt"（物理上的融化），用在資金語境下不自然且容易誤解。

✅ **修正：** "Unfreeze the funds."
> 在金融／行政語境中，解除資金限制應使用 "unfreeze"（或 "release / unlock"），"melt" 僅用於冰、雪等物理對象。

## 8. One Sentence to Remember

> **"The board voted to unfreeze the funds, releasing capital for the new expansion."**
